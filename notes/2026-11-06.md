The web app now opens instantly for returning visitors: Events, Profile and Chat paint from what's saved on the device instead of waiting on a cold server.

## Shipped

**Features**

- Faster cold opens on web: the app now keeps your own profile, points, badges, cheers, trophies, settings, ad-free status, ads, tags and chat list on the device and shows them right away (marked stale, then refreshed), and it uses the saved sign-in straight after load instead of waiting for the token refresh. Saved data is shown only to the person it was saved for, is deleted on sign-out or account switch, and expires after 7 days. On the mocked export with every server response delayed 8 s, Events went from 8.8 s to 0.6 s and Profile and Chat from 16.5 s to 0.5 s. Native apps don't do this yet (#187)
  - Prompted by your 1:23 AM open on Nov 6 taking ~15 s (2.6 s token refresh, then ~10.5 s of startup requests waiting on cold database connections).
  - Adds `scripts/shots/cold-open-flow.mjs` to measure a cold open, plus unit tests for the snapshot and the session provider.
