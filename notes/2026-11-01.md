A quiet Sunday: the Profile settings menu is now grouped into labelled sections, and the Events feed stops showing sessions that have already ended.

## Shipped

**Features**

- Profile: the 12 settings rows are now four labelled groups (Your activity, Preferences, Account, Help and legal). Same rows and links, only the order and grouping change (#159)

![profile-own, before → after](https://github.com/bryanw121/vteam/raw/release-notes/notes/assets/2026-11-01/profile-own.png)

**Fixes**

- Events feed: a session now leaves Today once it has ended (start + duration), checked every minute so it drops off without a refresh. In-progress sessions stay; tournaments and outside sessions with no posted end still stay for the rest of their day. Past events remain under Settings → History (#160). Client-side only; no screen change in the screenshot fixtures.
